Our Story

Building Timeless Value Since 2008

Costa Luxury Residences was founded with a singular conviction: that exceptional architecture, uncompromising craftsmanship and genuine respect for the Mediterranean landscape could coexist with sound investment fundamentals.

History

Three Decades of Coastal Architecture

2008

Foundation in Marbella

Costa Luxury Residences is established by a partnership of Spanish architects and Northern European investors, delivering its first boutique development of eight villas.

2014

Expansion to Costa Blanca

The company extends its portfolio to Altea and Dénia, introducing a design language attuned to the region's whitewashed heritage.

2019

Sustainability Charter Adopted

All new developments commit to A+ energy ratings, geothermal systems and certified sustainable timber sourcing.

2023

International Design Award

The Hills Collection in Benahavís receives the European Property Award for Best Architecture, Multiple Residence.

2026

€480M Portfolio Under Development

Costa Luxury Residences now manages six concurrent developments across the Costa Blanca and Costa del Sol.

Architectural Philosophy

Form Follows the Landscape

We believe a residence should never compete with its setting — every project begins with the orientation of the sun, the contour of the land and the framing of the sea. The result is architecture that feels inevitable: precise, restrained, and deeply connected to place.

Sustainability Commitment

Building Responsibly for Generations

Every development integrates passive solar design, geothermal climate control and greywater recycling. We work exclusively with FSC-certified timber and regional stone, minimising both embodied carbon and the visual footprint on the coastline.
